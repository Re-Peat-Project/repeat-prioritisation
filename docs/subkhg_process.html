Delineation

Sub-KHG are the catchments of the outlet channels, one per reach and bank, so divides and outlet canals are the boundaries. Small pieces are merged into a neighbour.

What to keep in mind

Facts from this run that limit how far the numbers can be trusted.

Peat depth has a floor at 50 cm. 37% of its valid pixels equal 50 cm; 13 of 25 sub-KHG have a mean at the floor, so thin peat is not resolved.
Block spacing is at its 2,000 m cap for 11 of 25 sub-KHG (canal slope under 0.0002): blocks needed there is canal length divided by the cap, not a terrain result.
Tree cover share is high (median 0.70) because WorldCover counts plantations as tree cover, so it cannot separate natural forest from acacia.
Neraca air failed its skill check on the 86 SiTMAT stations (no spatial skill), so its outputs are shown for reference only and have no weight in the need score.
Overlapping indicators: u_canal_density and f_reach (rho 0.884), i_peat_depth and f_lift (rho -0.87).
Not validated. No held-out outcome was set, the weights are defaults, Sentinel-2 is at 60 m, and the merge of small pieces is by longest shared divide, not by a hydrological hierarchy.
